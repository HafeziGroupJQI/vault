In addition to the [everyone checklist](../everyone/):

## Graduate School mandatory online orientation

The Graduate School runs an asynchronous **New Graduate Student Orientation**
on ELMS-Canvas: [gradschool.umd.edu/orientation](https://gradschool.umd.edu/orientation).
It applies to doctoral students, in-person master's students, and online
master's students (the modules differ by track). Complete it before or during
your first weeks.

## Health insurance (required)

Graduate students are required to have health insurance. As a graduate
assistant you must choose between:

- **SHIP** — the Student Health Insurance Plan (the default), or
- **State of Maryland employee benefits** — Kaiser / UnitedHealthcare / CareFirst
  plans, premiums deducted from your stipend. You have **60 days from your GA
  start date** to enroll, and must then waive out of SHIP.

Details and comparisons: [Graduate School health insurance pages](https://gradschool.umd.edu/health-insurance/Graduate_Assistants)
and the [FAQ](https://gradschool.umd.edu/health-insurance/health-insurance-faq).

## GTA orientation

If you hold a teaching assistantship, complete the TLTC's
[Graduate Student Orientation to Teaching](https://tltc.umd.edu/gatas/graduate-student-orientation-teaching)
(a ~90-minute digital course, plus optional in-person August workshops).

## RespectEdu (Title IX / sexual misconduct prevention training)

All new graduate students must complete the online **RespectEdu for Graduate
Students** course, typically about a month before the semester starts. As a GA
you are also a Responsible University Employee with reporting obligations.
See [OCRSM training](https://ocrsm.umd.edu/graduate-students).

<div class="callout callout--verify"><p>Whether non-graduate members must also
complete an equivalent training is unconfirmed.</p></div>
