## Research threads

- **Topological frequency combs:** nested combs and octave-spanning synthetic gauge fields.
- **InGaP nanophotonics:** second-harmonic generation in nonlinear resonator arrays.
- **Materials:** InGaP, TFLN, SiN, and anomalous quantum Hall lattices. See [[materials/index|Photonic materials]].
- **Optical computing:** MZI meshes, physical learning, and machine learning for ring lattices.
- **Measurement automation:** instrument control, motorized stages, and GDS-driven characterization.
- **TMD photonics:** transition-metal dichalcogenides on photonic structures.
