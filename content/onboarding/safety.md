The lab operates **Class 4 laser** systems (the amplified 1550 nm chain). The
rules below come from current lab practice; complete formal laser safety
training before working in the lab.

## Core rules

- **Physical beam blocks are the safety of last resort.** Flip-style laser
  blocks sit in the free-space path; for high-power measurements, if anything
  goes wrong, flip the block closed first. Any automation must respect these
  physical interlocks — software limits are not a substitute.
- **Respect power limits.** Know the maximum power allowed in each fiber path
  before turning anything up. The motorized gradient attenuator wheel
  (calibration in `Wheel_calibration`) is how power is set — verify with a
  power meter, don't assume.
- **Never crash the fiber into the chip.** Fiber-chip coupling is done under
  the microscope with sub-micron stages; approach slowly and watch the
  camera. At high power the fiber drifts thermally, so re-check alignment
  rather than compensating blindly.
- **Equipment placement conventions matter.** Some items live where they live
  for a reason (beam paths, interlocks). If you're unsure whether something
  can be moved, ask before moving it — and if a convention changes, write it
  down here instead of leaving it as oral tradition.
