The group uses the Atlantic Building and the adjoining Physical Sciences Complex (PSC) at 4296 Stadium Drive.
See [[places/index|Places]] for rooms and their occupants. Official campus directions are available from
[JQI](https://jqi.umd.edu/about/directions).

## Atlantic Building (Bldg 224)

Atlantic contains JQI offices, some laboratories, the JQI front office in Room 2207, and the group kitchen in Room 2366.

## Physical Sciences Complex (PSC)

PSC contains the basement and subbasement laboratories. Finance, payroll, and HR are on the ground floor.

## Finding your way in

The north-facing entrance can be confusing. Rooms numbered 24XX near that entrance are outside JQI;
follow signs toward JQI before looking for the 22XX and 23XX rooms. The exact after-hours entrance and
card-reader route still need confirmation.

## Access

- Request building and laboratory access separately using the [[onboarding/checklists/everyone|everyone checklist]].
- Ask a group lead to activate after-hours access on your UMD ID.
