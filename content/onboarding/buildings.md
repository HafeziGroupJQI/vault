The group lives in two adjoining buildings on Stadium Drive. Official
directions to JQI: [jqi.umd.edu/about/directions](https://jqi.umd.edu/about/directions).

## Atlantic Building (Bldg 224)

Atlantic is home to **JQI** and all of the group's **offices**, and also hosts
some of our **labs**. The **group kitchen** is here too. The JQI front office
is Room 2207. For who sits in which room, see the [Directory](../directory/)
rather than this page.

<div class="callout callout--verify"><p>Which specific labs are in Atlantic
(and their rooms) still needs to be written down by someone who knows —
add them here.</p></div>

## Physical Sciences Complex (PSC)

PSC adjoins Atlantic. Some of our **labs** are on the ground floor (possibly
one level below ground — see the flag below). **Finance, payroll, and HR** are
on the ground floor; this is where hire paperwork and reimbursements
physically happen.

<div class="callout callout--verify"><p>Two things to confirm: (1) whether the
labs are on the ground floor or the floor below it; (2) whether there is a
concierge/front desk to talk to for finance/payroll/HR or you should email
ahead for an appointment.</p></div>
