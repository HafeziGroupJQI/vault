The group's publications, one record per paper with authors, venue, DOI, and year, migrated from [hafezi.jqi.umd.edu/publications](https://hafezi.jqi.umd.edu/publications). Browse the [[publications/Publications.base|publications table]] (sortable, filter by year or research area), or the year tags such as [[tags/pub/2026|2026]]. A BibTeX file of everything here is `publications.bib` in this folder.

To add a paper, copy any record in this folder, fill in the frontmatter, and add a `pub/<year>` tag.
