J. E. Hoffman, J. A. Grover, Z. Kim, A. K. Wood, J. R. Anderson, A. J. Dragt, Mohammad Hafezi, C. J. Lobb, Luis A. Orozco, S. L. Rolston, Jacob M. Taylor, C. P. Vlahacos, F. C. Wellstood. *Revista Mexicana De Fisica* (2011).

We present a scheme to couple trapped Rb-87 atoms to a superconducting flux qubit through a magnetic dipole transition. We plan to trap atoms on the evanescent wave outside an ultrathin fiber to bring the atoms to less than 10 mu m above the surface of the superconductor. This hybrid setup lends itself to probing sources of decoherence in superconducting qubits. Our current plan has the intermediate goal of coupling the atoms to a superconducting LC resonator.
