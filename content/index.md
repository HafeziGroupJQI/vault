Welcome to the Hafezi Group! We are delighted to have you join us, and
congratulations on your achievement.

This site collects everything a new member needs to get started: who to
contact, how to get building and lab access, which trainings to complete, what
equipment we have, and where our research is heading within the broader scope
of topological photonics. It is continually contributed to and updated by
group members as we make progress — if you find something missing or out of
date, fix it (each page is a Markdown file; see the repository README).

## Why this site exists

New arrivals typically spend their first couple of months confused — and
everyone is confused about *different* things. Answering those questions
preemptively, the way the onboarding document our group modeled this on did at
MIT, frees that time for the good part: research. When something is sorted out
once (a new form, a policy email, a training contact), it gets filed here under
a titled section immediately, so the site accretes instead of needing a
writing campaign.

## How this fits together

The group's knowledge-sharing rests on three legs:

- **The past** — the journal-club series: ~10 key papers from the group (plus
  a few from collaborators and competitors), one per week, tutorial-style at
  first and student-led after that. See [Resources](resources/).
- **The future** — planning discussions and each member's 3 / 6 / 12-month
  goals. See [Current Directions](directions/).
- **The how** — this onboarding documentation: contacts, access, trainings,
  equipment, and operations. Start with the checklist for your role:
  [everyone](checklists/everyone/),
  [graduate students](checklists/graduate-students/),
  [postdocs](checklists/postdocs/), or
  [undergraduates](checklists/undergraduates/).
