The New York-based [Simons Foundation](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/) will be supporting our group activities for the next five years. Simons Investigator Awards in Mathematics, Physics, Astrophysics and Computer Science support outstanding theoretical scientists in their most productive years, when they are establishing creative new research directions, providing leadership to the field and effectively mentoring junior scientists.
