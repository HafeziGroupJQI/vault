This page covers the **Save to Google Drive** option on this website's pages.

**What it does.** When you choose Save to Google Drive, your browser asks Google for permission to create files in your Google Drive. It then uploads the page (as a PDF, a Google Doc, a Markdown file or a notebook file) straight from your browser to your Drive.

**What access it asks for.** Only Google's `drive.file` permission. It lets this site create files in your Drive and open the files it created. It can't see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

**What this site keeps.** Nothing. The Google sign-in and the upload happen in your browser. This site's servers never receive your Google account details, the permission Google grants, or the files you save, and nothing about them is stored or logged. Your browser remembers the email address of the Google account you chose, so Google doesn't ask which account again; the permission Google grants is never stored, and choosing "Use another Google account" in the Export menu, or signing out of this site, forgets the address.

**Removing access.** You can remove the permission at any time at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections): find Hafezi Group and delete its connection. Files you already saved stay in your Drive until you delete them.

**Google's part.** Google's handling of your account and Drive is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
