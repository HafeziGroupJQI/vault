Topology describes features that survive smooth changes to a system. We design optical systems whose connectivity produces robust states, then use them to study new physics and build photonic devices.

For background, see the group’s [Physics Today overview](https://doi.org/10.1063/PT.3.2394) and [review in Reviews of Modern Physics](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.91.015006).
