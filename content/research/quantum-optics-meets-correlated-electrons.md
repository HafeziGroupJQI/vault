We use precise control of photons, spins, excitons, and phonons to prepare and probe correlated electronic states. The goal is to bring quantum-optical measurement techniques to many-body and topological materials.

Read the group’s [Nature perspective](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04726-w) for an overview.
